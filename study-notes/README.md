# ACCA LW-GLO study notes (September 2026 – June 2027)

Study notes for **Corporate and Business Law – Global (LW-GLO)**. They go with
the 29-episode animated series *The Law Behind the Deal* in
[`animation/`](../animation/README.md).

The notes follow the ACCA *Syllabus and Study Guide, September 2026 to June 2027*
section by section. They also draw on the OpenTuition LW-GLO lecture notes you
supplied, and on primary sources checked online: UNCITRAL, the ICC, the UN
treaty collection, UK legislation, and ACCA's own guidance. Every file ends with
its sources, and [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md) lists them all.

> **Important.** These are learning aids. The characters, companies and facts in
> the episodes are fictional. Company and insolvency law in LW-GLO follows the
> UK model (Companies Act 2006, Insolvency Act 1986 and related Acts). The
> "Local lens" boxes on Tanzania and Kenya are for context only, and the exam
> does not test them. Always check against the current ACCA syllabus,
> examiner's reports and technical articles before you rely on a point.

## The exam at a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Format | 2-hour computer-based exam, all questions compulsory |
| Section A | 25 × 2-mark and 20 × 1-mark objective test questions (70%) |
| Section B | 5 × 6-mark multi-task questions (30%) |
| Pass mark | 50% |
| Syllabus changes for 2026–27 | None |

## How to study with the series

1. Watch an episode. At each **Quick check**, pause the video before the
   countdown ends and choose your answer.
2. Read the matching notes file. Write the "exam traps" from memory, then check
   them.
3. Try the practice questions at the end of each notes file.
4. Do timed question practice from an approved exam kit. Objective test
   technique matters as much as knowing the law.
