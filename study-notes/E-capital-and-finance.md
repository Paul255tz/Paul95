# E. Capital and the financing of companies

**Episodes 18–20** · Syllabus E1 (share capital), E2 (loan capital), E3
(capital maintenance and dividend law). UK Companies Act 2006 and Insolvency
Act 1986 model.

## E1. Share capital

**Types of capital (E1a):**

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Nominal (par) value | The face value of each share, e.g. $1 |
| Issued / allotted | The nominal value of shares actually issued |
| Called up | The amount the company has asked shareholders to pay |
| Paid up | The amount actually paid |
| Uncalled | The amount not yet called. This is the members' remaining liability |
| Reserve capital | Uncalled capital that a special resolution says can be called only on winding up |
| Authorised capital | **Abolished** by CA 2006 as a requirement. Articles may still set a cap |
| Market value | What a buyer will pay. Unrelated to nominal value |

**Classes of shares (E1b):**

- **Ordinary** shares: usually voting; dividend after preference shares; share
  any **surplus** on winding up.
- **Preference** shares: a fixed dividend paid first, **presumed cumulative**;
  usually **no vote**; priority for return of capital **only if the terms say
  so**; no share in surplus unless stated.
- **Redeemable** shares: bought back on set terms. They can only be issued if
  there are also non-redeemable shares in issue. They are financed from
  distributable profits or a fresh issue.
- **Treasury shares:** shares bought back out of distributable profits may be
  **held in treasury** instead of cancelled. They carry **no votes and no
  dividends**. They can later be **sold**, used for an **employee share
  scheme**, or **cancelled**. On cancellation the company files a return with a
  statement of capital.

**Variation of class rights (ss630–635):**

- Follow the articles. If they say nothing, the variation needs either the
  **written consent of holders of 75%** of the class (by nominal value) or a
  **special resolution at a separate class meeting**.
- Holders of **at least 15%** of the class who did not consent may apply to
  court **within 21 days** to cancel the variation.
- *White v Bristol Aeroplane* (1953): issuing more shares, which dilutes a
  class's votes, is **not** a variation of its rights.

**Allotment (E1c):**

- Directors need **authority** to allot (s549):
  - a private company with one class may allot unless the articles forbid it
    (s550)
  - otherwise authority comes from the articles or an **ordinary resolution**,
    for up to **5 years** (s551)
- **Pre-emption** (s561): new **equity shares issued for cash** must first be
  offered to existing ordinary shareholders, **pro rata**, for at least **14
  days**.
  - It does not apply to **bonus** issues, **non-cash** issues or employee
    schemes.
  - It can be **disapplied by special resolution**, or by a private company's
    articles.
- A **return of allotment** goes to the registrar within **1 month**.

| Rights issue | Bonus (capitalisation) issue |
|---|---|
| Offered to existing members pro rata, usually at a discount to market price | Free, fully paid shares to existing members |
| **Raises new cash** | **No cash**: reserves (including share premium) are turned into share capital |
| Rights are often renounceable, so they can be sold | — |

**Discount and premium (E1d):**

- Shares may **not** be allotted **below nominal value** (s580; *Ooregum Gold
  Mining v Roper*, 1892). The allottee must pay the discount plus interest.
- Payment may be in **money or money's worth**.
  - A private company's directors value non-cash assets honestly (*Re Wragg*,
    1897).
  - A **plc** needs an **independent valuation** of non-cash consideration, and
    cannot accept an undertaking to do work or perform services.
  - A plc's shares must be at least **¼ paid up** plus the whole premium.
- **Premium:** the excess over nominal value goes to the **share premium
  account** (s610). It is treated as capital. It may only be used to pay up
  **bonus shares** and to write off the **expenses of, and commission on, that
  issue**. It can also be reduced like capital.

## E2. Loan capital

- **Borrowing powers (E2a):** a trading company has an implied power to borrow,
  and with unrestricted objects the power is general unless the articles
  restrict it. The directors exercise it. A **plc** needs its **trading
  certificate** before borrowing.
- **Debentures (E2b):** a debenture is a document acknowledging a debt. It may
  be a single debenture, a **series** (ranking *pari passu*), or **debenture
  stock** held through a trustee under a **trust deed**. It may be secured or
  unsecured.

**Loan capital v share capital (E2c):**

| Debenture holder | Shareholder |
|---|---|
| A **creditor** | A **member** (owner) |
| Interest payable whether or not there are profits | Dividends only from distributable profits |
| Usually no vote | Usually votes |
| Repaid before members on winding up; secured holders can enforce | Repaid last |
| May be issued at a discount; freely redeemable | No discount; buy-backs controlled |
| Interest is tax-deductible for the company | Dividends are not |

**Charges (E2d):**

- A **fixed charge** attaches to **specific** assets (land, buildings, major
  machinery). The company cannot deal with them without the lender's consent.
- A **floating charge** (*Re Yorkshire Woolcombers*, 1903):
  - over a **class** of assets, present and future
  - that **changes** in the ordinary course of business
  - which the company may deal with until **crystallisation**
- **Substance beats the label:** *Re Spectrum Plus* (2005). A "fixed" charge
  over book debts was floating, because the company could freely use the
  proceeds.
- **Crystallisation** happens on liquidation, ceasing business, the
  appointment of a receiver or administrator, or an event specified in the
  charge.
- **Weaknesses of a floating charge:**
  - it ranks after fixed charges, liquidation **expenses** and **preferential**
    creditors, and is subject to the **prescribed part**
  - one created within **12 months** before insolvency (**2 years** for a
    connected person) is invalid except for **new value** (IA s245)
  - a fixed charge to an existing creditor within 6 months (2 years if
    connected) may be a **preference** (IA s239)
- **Negative pledge** clause: forbids the company to create later charges with
  priority. It binds a later chargee who **knew** of it.

**Priority:**

1. Fixed charge before floating charge, unless a negative pledge is known.
2. Between charges of the same type, the earlier **created** ranks first,
   provided each was registered on time.
3. Priority can be changed by **agreement**.

**Registration (E2e):**

- Register at Companies House within **21 days**, starting the day after the
  charge is **created** (s859A).
- The registrar's **certificate** is conclusive.
- If not registered, the charge is **void against a liquidator, administrator
  and creditors** (s859H). The debt remains, now **unsecured**, and becomes
  immediately **repayable**.
- The court may extend the time (s859F).
- The company keeps copies of charge instruments for inspection.

## E3. Capital maintenance and dividends

**The doctrine (E3a):** capital is the **creditors' buffer** and must not be
returned to members except as the law allows (*Trevor v Whitworth*, 1887). The
buffer is share capital plus **undistributable reserves**: share premium,
capital redemption reserve, the excess of accumulated **unrealised** profits
over unrealised losses, and any other reserve the law or articles say cannot be
distributed.

**Reducing capital:**

- Reasons: capital lost or unrepresented by assets; cancelling unpaid
  liability; returning surplus capital.
- **Private company:** a **special resolution supported by a solvency
  statement**. **Every director** states the company can pay its debts for the
  next **12 months**. Making the statement without reasonable grounds is an
  offence.
- **Any company** (and every **plc**): a **special resolution confirmed by the
  court**. Creditors may object.
- A plc that falls below £50,000 must re-register as private, unless the court
  orders otherwise.

**Other safeguards:**

- Buy-backs and redemptions come from **distributable profits** or a fresh
  issue, with transfers to the **capital redemption reserve**. A private company
  may use capital, with a solvency statement.
- **Financial assistance** for the purchase of a **plc's** own shares is
  prohibited (s678).
- **Serious loss of capital** (s656): if a plc's net assets fall to **half or
  less** of its called-up share capital, the directors must call a general
  meeting within **28 days**.

**Dividends (E3b):**

- **All companies (s830):** dividends come only from profits available for the
  purpose: **accumulated realised profits less accumulated realised losses**.
  There is no distinction between capital and revenue.
- **Public companies (s831):** after the distribution, **net assets must be at
  least** called-up share capital plus undistributable reserves. So net
  **unrealised losses** also count.
- They are measured on the **relevant accounts**.
- Under the model articles, members declare a final dividend by **ordinary
  resolution**, never more than the directors recommend. Directors may pay
  **interim** dividends. A declared dividend becomes a **debt** owed to the
  member.
- **Unlawful dividends:**
  - a member who **knew or had reasonable grounds to believe** the dividend was
    unlawful must repay it (s847; *It's a Wrap v Gula*, 2006)
  - directors who authorised it may be **personally liable** (*Re Exchange
    Banking Co, Flitcroft's Case*, 1882)

**Worked example (private company):**

| | $ |
|---|---|
| Accumulated realised profits | 200,000 |
| Accumulated realised losses | (80,000) |
| **Maximum dividend** | **120,000** |

## Exam traps

- Pre-emption applies to **equity shares for cash** only.
- A **bonus** issue raises no cash. A **rights** issue does.
- Share premium uses are **narrow**. It is not available for dividends.
- **Fixed beats floating.** A charge registered late is void against the
  liquidator, but the debt survives.
- The plc dividend test is **two-stage**: profits, then the net assets test.
