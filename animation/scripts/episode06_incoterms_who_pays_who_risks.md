## Scene 3: Any-mode rules on the map `1:31`

*Route map (risk and costs) · Syllabus B1(c)*

**On screen**

**Heading:** Any mode of transport
- **EXW** Ex Works: Buyer does almost everything
- **FCA** Free Carrier: Risk passes on handover to the carrier
- **CPT** Carriage Paid To: Seller pays carriage, risk passed at first carrier
- **CIP** Carriage & Insurance Paid To: Plus all-risks insurance (ICC A), 110%
- **DAP** Delivered at Place: Risk passes on arrival, ready for unloading
- **DPU** Delivered at Place Unloaded: Seller unloads too
- **DDP** Delivered Duty Paid: Seller clears import and pays duties

**Script**

- **PROFESSOR LEX:** EXW, Ex Works. The seller just makes the goods available at its own premises. The buyer loads, clears export, and bears all risk from there.
- **PROFESSOR LEX:** FCA, Free Carrier. The seller clears export and hands the goods to the buyer's carrier. Risk passes on that handover.
- **PROFESSOR LEX:** CPT, Carriage Paid To. The seller pays the carriage all the way to the named destination, but risk still passes when the goods reach the first carrier.
- **PROFESSOR LEX:** CIP is the same, plus insurance. Under the 2020 rules, the seller must buy all-risks cover, for at least the price plus ten percent.
- **PROFESSOR LEX:** DAP, Delivered at Place. The seller bears the risk until the goods arrive, ready for unloading, at the named place.
- **PROFESSOR LEX:** DPU, Delivered at Place Unloaded. The only rule where the seller must also unload the goods.
- **PROFESSOR LEX:** And DDP, Delivered Duty Paid. Maximum obligation for the seller, who even clears import and pays the duties.

## Scene 4: Sea-only rules on the map `2:42`

*Route map (risk and costs) · Syllabus B1(c)*

**On screen**

**Heading:** Sea and inland waterway only
- **FAS** Free Alongside Ship: Risk passes alongside the vessel
- **FOB** Free On Board: Risk passes once on board
- **CFR** Cost and Freight: Freight paid to destination port, risk passed on board
- **CIF** Cost, Insurance and Freight: Plus minimum cover (ICC C), 110%
- Caption: C terms: two critical points

**Script**

- **PROFESSOR LEX:** Now the four sea rules. FAS, Free Alongside Ship. Risk passes when the goods are placed alongside the vessel at the port of shipment.
- **PROFESSOR LEX:** FOB, Free On Board. The buyer books the ship. Risk passes once the goods are on board.
- **PROFESSOR LEX:** CFR, Cost and Freight. The seller pays the freight to the destination port, but risk has already passed on board, at the port of shipment.
- **PROFESSOR LEX:** CIF adds insurance. But here the minimum is only basic cover, again for the price plus ten percent.
- **NIA:** So with every C term, the seller pays further than it carries the risk. Two critical points!

*Teaching board · Syllabus B1(c)*

**Heading:** Five classic traps
- C terms: pay far, risk passes early
- CIP = all-risks · CIF = minimum cover
- EXW: buyer exports · DDP: seller imports
- Containers? Prefer FCA, CPT, CIP
- DPU: the only rule where seller unloads

**Script**

- **THE AUDITOR:** Let me flag the classic exam traps.
- **THE AUDITOR:** Under C terms, the seller pays to the destination, but risk passes at shipment.
- **THE AUDITOR:** CIP needs all-risks insurance. CIF only needs minimum cover.
- **THE AUDITOR:** Under EXW, the buyer clears export. Under DDP, the seller clears import.
- **THE AUDITOR:** For goods in containers, FCA, CPT or CIP usually fit better than FOB, CFR or CIF.
- **THE AUDITOR:** And DPU is the only rule that makes the seller unload.
