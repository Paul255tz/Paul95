About me

I care about making hard subjects simple. Law textbooks can feel dry, so I turn the key ideas into stories: a young analyst, a startup founder, a wise professor and a talking shipping container working through real business problems together.

My current project follows the ACCA Corporate and Business Law (LW-GLO) syllabus. Each episode takes one big topic and explains it in about five minutes, in both 2D and 3D.
